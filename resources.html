About This Project

This website was created as Assignment 2 for COMP-213 Web Interface Design (Section 006) at the British Columbia Institute of Technology (BCIT), Fall 2023. The project explores key environmental protection and climate change topics, curating information from credible international organizations and scientific institutions. All content has been sourced from publicly available materials and is attributed below.
