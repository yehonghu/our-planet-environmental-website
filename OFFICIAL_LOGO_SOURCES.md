## Presentation Rule

All true official Logo assets appear in an identical white presentation frame with shared padding and maximum dimensions. The frame standardizes surrounding space only; it does not redraw, recolour, crop, or otherwise alter the official identity. Every entry retains its direct link to the relevant organization’s official website.
