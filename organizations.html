Global Partners

Organizations
making a difference

These leading institutions are at the forefront of environmental protection and climate action worldwide. Visit their websites to learn more and get involved.

ASEZ

International Volunteer Organization

ASEZ (Save the Earth from A to Z) is an international volunteer organization composed of university students from the World Mission Society Church of God. They conduct environmental campaigns and initiatives worldwide, focusing on practical action to address climate change and environmental degradation. Their grassroots approach mobilizes young people to take direct action in their communities.

IUCN — International Union for Conservation of Nature

International Conservation Authority

The International Union for Conservation of Nature (IUCN) is the world's largest and most diverse environmental network, harnessing the experience, resources and reach of its more than 1,400 Member organizations and the input of some 15,000 experts. IUCN is best known for its Red List of Threatened Species, the world's most comprehensive inventory of species' conservation status. Their work spans biodiversity conservation, natural resource management, and environmental law.

Visit Website ↗

Global Affairs Canada

Government — International Climate Finance

Global Affairs Canada manages Canada's diplomatic and consular relations, trade negotiations, and international development and humanitarian assistance. Canada is working to advance global environmental protection, with international assistance focusing on countries with a high impact on climate change. Canada is committed to supporting developing countries in their transition to a low-carbon, climate-resilient world through significant climate finance commitments.

Visit Website ↗

NASA Climate — Vital Signs of the Planet

Scientific Research Agency

NASA is a global leader in climate research and Earth science. While NASA's role does not include setting climate policy or prescribing particular responses or solutions to climate change, its responsibilities do include providing the robust scientific data needed to understand climate change and evaluate strategies for mitigation and adaptation. Their Earth-observing satellites provide critical data on temperature, sea level, ice coverage, and atmospheric composition.

Visit Website ↗

David Suzuki Foundation

Canadian Environmental Organization

The David Suzuki Foundation works through science and public education to protect the diversity of nature and our quality of life, now and for the future. Founded by renowned scientist and broadcaster David Suzuki, the organization focuses on climate solutions, biodiversity conservation, and sustainable communities. They work with government, businesses, and individuals to find solutions that allow humans to live in balance with the natural world.

Visit Website ↗

UNEP — United Nations Environment Programme

United Nations Agency

The United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP) is the leading global environmental authority that sets the global environmental agenda, promotes the coherent implementation of the environmental dimension of sustainable development within the United Nations system, and serves as an authoritative advocate for the global environment. UNEP has shown that the problem of plastic pollution does not exist in isolation — the environmental, social, economic and health risks of plastics need to be assessed in conjunction with other environmental stressors.
